Programmable treasury settlement on Arc.

Lock USDC in an onchain vault against a release condition. The moment the condition is met, settlement runs itself across currencies and chains. The contract decides whether the money moves. Nobody signs after the trigger.

Every claim on this page links to a transaction on a public testnet explorer. Built for the Circle brief on stablecoin-native DeFi on Arc. Testnet only.

One settlement, every moving part

A cross-chain payment that runs itself, honest about its one trust window.

A policy releases 0.5 USDC on Arc and pays a recipient on Base Sepolia. The executor burns through CCTP v2, and Circle's forwarder mints straight to the recipient, so the recipient needs no gas token on the destination chain.

The recipient received more than the policy amount, never less: the forwarder fee is deducted from the mint, so the executor grosses up the burn from its own balance. The fee is flat, 0.053301 USDC, a rounding error at treasury size. The custody gap, the seconds the executor holds funds between release and payout, is timestamped on every settlement rather than glossed over.

How it is built

The Circle stack, each part doing one job.

Nothing here is a logo for a product Covenant does not use.

Arc
USDC-native L1 with sub-second finality. The vault deploys for six cents, priced in dollars, with no separate volatile gas token.
USDC
The native gas token and the settlement asset. Amounts, fees, and deploy cost are all in dollars.
Circle Wallets
Developer-controlled treasury, executor, and recipient wallets, behind one interface for a later move to user-controlled wallets.
App Kit
The FX swap, USDC to EURC, and the payout send, run server-side with idempotent settlement state.
CCTP v2
Native cross-chain USDC by burn and mint, with a forwarder so the recipient needs no gas on the destination chain.
Gateway
Fund an Arc policy from a unified USDC balance sourced on another chain, with no manual bridge.
Pyth
A pull oracle deployed on Arc, reachable with no credentials. It drives the depeg condition above through a standard aggregator interface.
PolicyVault
The onchain contract that holds the USDC and enforces the release condition. It refuses to move funds whose condition is unmet.
